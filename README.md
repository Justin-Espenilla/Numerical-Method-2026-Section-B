## 2. How to download the materials

**The quick way (no software needed).**
Click the green **Code** button at the top of this page, then **Download ZIP**.
Extract it anywhere and open `materials/index.html` in a browser.

**The proper way (recommended once you are comfortable).**
Click **Fork** at the top right to make your own copy of this repository. You
will need a fork in order to submit anyway — see the submission guide.

Every page in `materials/` opens on its own with no internet connection. The
`.py` files need Python, NumPy and Matplotlib installed; that is what
Assignment 0 sets up.

## 4. Using AI assistants

AI assistants are **permitted and expected** in this course. Concealing that
use is academic dishonesty.

Every assignment from Laboratory 1 onward requires an **AI Prompt Log**: a Word
document recording every prompt you sent, word for word, what came back, how
you checked it, and what you corrected. It is worth 15 to 30 % of the
assignment mark, and a missing log is a zero rather than a deduction.

The full policy and the four-level marking rubric are in
`materials/grading.html`.

## 5. Rules for this repository

1. **Work only inside your own folder** in `submissions/`. Editing another
   student's folder, or anything in `materials/`, is treated as academic
   dishonesty.
2. **Never commit another student's work**, even to help them.
3. Commit history is part of the record. A submission whose entire content
   appears in one commit two minutes before the deadline invites a bench check.
4. Ask questions as **Issues** on this repository rather than by e-mail, so
   that the whole section benefits from the answer.
