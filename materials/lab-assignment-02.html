Maximum deflection of a simply supported beam of rectangular cross-section under a central point load:

δ = P L348 E I    with    I = b h312

The serviceability limit is L/360. Your budget for extra measurement is ₱ 15,000.

Part A — The sensitivity engine 25 pts

  1. Implement partial(func, params, key, rel_h) with a scaled centred-difference perturbation, and sensitivity_table(func, params) returning results sorted by |S|.
  2. Compute δ at the nominal values. Report it in millimetres and state whether the beam passes L/360.
  3. Produce the full sensitivity table: parameter, value, ∂δ/∂x with units, and dimensionless S.
  4. Verify against the physics. The model is a product of powers. Write down the exponent of each parameter by inspection and show that your numerical S values reproduce them to at least four significant figures. Any parameter that does not match is a bug — find it before you submit.
  5. Derive ∂δ/∂h analytically by hand and compare with your numerical value. Show the derivation.

Part B — The perturbation sweep 20 pts

  1. For ∂δ/∂E, sweep rel_h from 10−1 to 10−15 and plot the relative error against the analytical value on a log-log axis.
  2. Report the optimal rel_h and the minimum error achieved. Explain both arms of the curve, in the vocabulary of truncation and round-off error.
  3. Repeat for a forward-difference partial on the same parameter, on the same axes. Which method has the lower minimum, and which reaches its minimum at a larger perturbation? Explain why.
  4. State the rel_h you will use for the rest of the assignment and justify it in one sentence.

Part C — The recommendation 30 pts

  1. Propagate the uncertainties. Report δ ± worst-case and δ ± RSS, both in millimetres. Explain to a non-specialist client, in two sentences, why the two numbers differ.
  2. Build a tornado chart: horizontal bars of each parameter's individual contribution to the RSS total, sorted largest at the top. Label the bars with the percentage of total variance each contributes.
  3. Run a Monte Carlo with 20,000 samples. Plot the histogram of δ and overlay the RSS-predicted normal distribution. Report the Monte Carlo mean and standard deviation, and compare with your RSS number — agreement or disagreement, quantified.
  4. Spend the money. For each parameter, compute how much the total RSS uncertainty would fall if that parameter's uncertainty were halved. Combine with the cost table and the ₱15,000 budget to make one concrete recommendation. State the reduction achieved, in millimetres and in percent.
  5. Compute the probability that the beam fails the L/360 limit, using your Monte Carlo samples. Then state what you would tell the project manager in one sentence.
Where marks are lost in Part C

Recommending the parameter with the largest S without checking its uncertainty or its price. A parameter can be highly sensitive and already precisely known, in which case measuring it again buys nothing. The deliverable is a decision, and a decision needs all three: sensitivity, uncertainty, cost.

Part D — AI Prompt Log 25 pts

Log every prompt verbatim in the template. This assignment weights the log more heavily than Lab 01 because the required experiment is harder.

Required prompt experiment — catching a confident wrong answer

Ask an AI assistant to perform the engineering judgement, not just the code:

I have a beam deflection formula delta = P*L^3/(48*E*I) with I = b*h^3/12.
P, L, E, b, h are all uncertain. Which measurement should I improve first?

Then verify its answer against your own numbers, and answer in the log:

  1. Did it account for the magnitude of each uncertainty, or only the exponents? Most models answer on sensitivity alone. Show the numbers that prove which it did.
  2. Did it ask you for the uncertainties or the costs before answering? If it answered without them, say what that tells you about accepting AI recommendations in engineering practice.
  3. Write a second, improved prompt that supplies the uncertainty and cost tables and demands the reasoning be shown. Compare the two answers.
  4. State your final recommendation and whether the AI agreed with it. If you followed the AI's answer, say what evidence convinced you. If you overruled it, say what it missed.
